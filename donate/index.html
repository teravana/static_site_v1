Frequently Asked questions

How will my donation be used?

All administrative expenses are covered by donations from its trustees, so you can be confident that 100% of your donation goes to supporting programs that have a direct impact. This includes our reforestation efforts planting champion trees in Northern California, educational programming for the youth on ecology & our community initiatives in Guatemala with our program called Wellkind. 

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, your donations to TERAVANA, LLC are fully tax-deductible in the U.S., as contributions to an approved 501(c)(3) charity formed in 2020 with the exclusive purpose of supporting TERAVANA and its projects. TERAVANA is part of Teravana Trust (f/k/a WELLKIND Trust) for tax purposes: ID: 83-4395282. No goods or services were received for this donation.

You will receive a receipt via email stating the amount and date of your donation.

How secure is your online donation form?

All transactions made through our online donation form is 100% secure and encrypted by SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption technology.
