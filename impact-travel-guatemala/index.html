Teravana Global Engagement & Travel

Travel with Purpose: Wellkind Guatemala 2026

As part of Teravana’s international focus, we invite California high schools and colleges to join us on a transformative journey into the heart of Guatemala. Our 9-day travel experience weaves together environmental science, cultural immersion, and real-world contribution through our sister initiative, Wellkind Guatemala. This isn’t a vacation, it’s an invitation to connect with land, people, and purpose.

Next Dates: Spring/Summer 2026
Destination: Lake Atitlán and Pacific Coast, Guatemala

Why This Experience Matters

This program is designed for students to step into an immersive, high-impact learning experience beyond the classroom. Rooted in environmental education and global connection, the journey helps young people grow as learners, leaders, and contributors.
What Students Will Experience:

Ecology in Action

Explore reforestation sites, native seed nurseries, and biodiverse ecosystems with local conservationists.

Cultural Connection

Engage in deep exchange with Indigenous leaders, youth, and community organizations to better understand Guatemala's complex history and vibrant present.

Spanish in Context

Practice Spanish through real-world interaction and supported conversation, building confidence in an immersive setting.

Purposeful Service

Get your hands in the soil restoring forests, stabilizing hillsides, and learning regenerative agriculture.

Reflective Leadership

Participate in daily group reflection and facilitated discussion that encourages personal growth and purpose-driven thinking.

Itinerary Overview

Day 1

Arrival in Guatemala and overnight in Antigua

Day 2–4

Lake Atitlán immersion with ecology projects and cultural exchange

Day 5–6

Pacific Coast exploration and environmental education

Day 7–8

Return to Antigua for a final reflection and farewell before departing the next day for California.

For High Schools

This experience helps fulfill required community service hours while offering students the chance to:

  • Deepen their understanding of science, civic responsibility, and climate justice
  • Strengthen their Spanish skills in real-life contexts
  • Grow as global citizens with empathy, confidence, and curiosity

All student safety standards, accommodations, and travel logistics are fully vetted and supported by our experienced team in-country.

Service hours: Over 15 hours of certified, verified service work that meets high school graduation and extracurricular goals.

For Colleges and University Programs

We collaborate with professors, global studies programs, and honors courses to align this trip with academic frameworks. We can provide:

  • Support in integrating coursework pre-trip, during travel, and post-return
  • Collaboration on field-based learning outcomes tied to environmental studies, anthropology, education, and more
  • Collaboration on field-based learning outcomes tied to environmental studies, anthropology, education, and more

Many colleges use this trip as:

  • A capstone or practicum course experience
  • A global field course with optional academic credit
  • An honors program immersion trip

Let us know how we can support your curricular integration—we’re happy to provide templates, examples, and reflection guides.

Impact That Lasts

Student Travelers

140+ students have traveled over the last 2 years, primarily from public and private high schools across Idaho, including Boise and Bishop Kelly.

Adult Travelers

25+ adult travelers from corporate and Rotary groups have joined impact trips during that time.

Funds Raised

Over $40,000 raised directly for Teravana's grassroots nonprofit initiative in Guatemala since 2023.

Trees Planted

Water Projects

Two school water access projects completed, improving infrastructure for local students.

Local Partners

10+ years of in-country operations via trusted, insured local partners handling logistics, safety, and nonprofit relationships.

School Engagement

Program has engaged 6 or more high schools since launch, with repeat participation from key school partners.

Trip Impact

All trips include service learning, cultural immersion, and environmental impact, helping students grow as global citizens and emerging leaders.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a vacation?

Not at all. While we explore breathtaking places, the focus is on service, education, and personal growth. Students return transformed.

Who is this trip for?

Primarily high school and college students in California interested in ecology, culture, service learning, or global citizenship. Teachers and faculty often join as group leaders.

Is it safe?

Yes. All accommodations, transport, and staff are vetted. We operate with clear safety protocols and have bilingual guides on-site throughout the trip.

How is this different from other student travel programs?

This experience is rooted in real partnership with local communities. Projects are ongoing and community-led, not “voluntourism.”

What kind of service do students do?

Reforestation, watershed protection, native seed collection, and sustainable gardening—all supervised by local experts.

Can this trip fulfill service hours or credit?

Yes. We offer documentation for over 15 hours of hands-on service, and we work with professors and teachers to align the program to course objectives.

Who runs the trip on the ground?

Somos Impact Travel is our trusted in-country logistics partner. They have over 10 years of experience operating student trips in Latin America.

How do we get started?

Reach out using the contact form below. We’ll guide you through the planning process, curriculum alignment, and group prep.

About Wellkind Guatemala

Wellkind Guatemala is a program of Teravana, created to support ecological regeneration and community resilience in the highlands of Guatemala. By joining this trip, you directly contribute to their work restoring forests, watersheds, and Indigenous food systems.

Let’s Explore This Together

If you’re a teacher, professor, or student group leader interested in this journey, we’re here to help bring it to life.
